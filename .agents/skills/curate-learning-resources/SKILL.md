# Curate Learning Resources

Read `AGENTS.md`, `resources/README.md`, the current lesson and `learning/progress.md`.

1. Identify the exact concept, prerequisites and 120-minute study budget.
2. Prefer authoritative, legal, free resources: MIT OCW, university open materials, All About Circuits, Feedback Systems, CODESYS documentation, Siemens SCE, Ignition University, Modbus, OPC Foundation, Python, Node-RED, Grafana and vendor docs.
3. For every recommended resource give **title, publisher, exact URL if verified, section/chapter or search term, level, language and what to learn**.
4. Distinguish **verified direct lesson** from general resource landing page or unverified target. Do not invent chapters, PDF pages, product version numbers or prices.
5. Assign one original worked example and a separate independently solvable practice problem. Give numerical inputs, units, expected deliverables and self-check criteria.
6. List the simulator and setup needed, including a software-only option.
7. When sources conflict, favor official version-specific documentation, explain uncertainty and offer an experiment to verify.
8. Avoid copying substantial copyrighted textbook content; provide links and original summaries/problems.
