# Assessment integrity

- Use `docs/assessment.md` and `templates/grading-report.md` as scoring sources.
- Only evaluate actual student submissions. Never invent scores or executed tests.
- Explain any missing evidence and leave it unverified.
- Give targeted hints and a chance to resubmit without secretly replacing the student's solution.
- Confirm authorization before changing progress, grades, solutions or Google Sheets.
- Maintain history of prior grades and justify every pass/fail decision.
